Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy describes what the public relay and browser editor at edit.piconic.ai (“the service”) keeps about you. It is run by kobaken, an individual (kobaken.co); contact kobaken@piconic.ai. The details, kept up to date with the code, are in the public relay notes and the security design.

Your documents

Document updates, comments and pasted images are encrypted on your device before they are sent. The key is in the part of the link after #, which your browser never sends to the service. The service relays encrypted data and cannot read it. It does not store document text. Your files stay on the host’s computer.

What the service keeps

  • Encrypted images are kept while the room’s host is connected. The room deletes them when the host leaves. If a deletion fails, a storage rule expires them one day after upload, and Cloudflare removes them typically within a further day.
  • Per room: a hash of the room’s admission token, and the number and total size of the stored images, deleted when the host leaves; and the time the host first connected, kept after the room ends so that the room ID cannot be used again. The room’s storage keeps 30 days of history for point-in-time recovery.

Logs

The service logs requests with Cloudflare Workers Logs, kept for 7 days. A log entry has the time and path of a request (the path contains the room ID), its status, and the details Cloudflare adds, which include country, city, postal code, coordinates and network of the request. Events in a room, including each relayed message, are logged with their time. The content of the messages is encrypted and is not logged.

Not yet confirmed: whether these logs also keep the client’s IP address, and which request headers they keep, in particular two tokens that cannot decrypt anything. This page will be updated when it is confirmed.

Cloudflare

The service runs on Cloudflare, which handles every request and sees your IP address and everything the service sees. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

In your browser

The editor’s own code sets no cookies and loads no analytics or tracking scripts. Not yet confirmed: whether Cloudflare features on the piconic.ai zone add a cookie or script of their own. The editor keeps in your browser’s local storage only the name you typed and display preferences, never a room ID, key or document content. Your browser history keeps the full link, key included.

Third parties

The Markdown preview does not load images or videos from other sites. A link to a video player or to a public file on GitHub loads only after you click it (or choose “Always load” for that provider), and then the provider sees your IP address. The service sees nothing of it. The pedit command line tool itself contacts api.github.com once a day to check for a newer release, with no file name, room or key.

Your requests

The service cannot read your documents, so it cannot show or delete them. To ask what is held about a room, or to ask for a room to be blocked or its data removed, write to kobaken@piconic.ai with the room ID (the 22 characters after /r/, without the # and what follows).
