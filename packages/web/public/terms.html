What the service is

The service relays end-to-end encrypted edits between people who edit the same files with pedit. It is free, provided as is, and has no service level agreement. Rooms have the limits listed in the public relay notes, such as a 24-hour session length.

Rooms or networks that break these rules may be blocked.

Anyone you give a link to can read the files, and can edit them if the link allows editing. A guest who joins from the command line saves a copy on their computer. Closing a room does not erase copies that participants have made. Share links only with people you trust.

Reporting abuse

Send the room link, or just the room ID (the 22 characters after /r/), to abuse@piconic.ai. Leave out the # and what follows unless you want the operator to see the content: the full link also lets them edit. The operator can block a room by its ID without seeing its content, which is encrypted. To report a vulnerability instead, see SECURITY.md.
